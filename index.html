Hall of Meaningful Experiences

A 30-day invitation to do something that makes you feel alive.

Join the 30-day challenge: Sep 1 to Sep 30, 2026

“Perhaps the most meaningful things in life are the things we do without a deadline, without a reward and without anyone watching. We do them simply because something inside us wants to. And perhaps, that is where we find our HoME.”

Hall of Meaning Experiences is a project to bring alive the passion that is buried amongst the deadlines and routines. It is a simple invitation to dedicate some time to what you love. The project runs from September 1 to September 30, 30 days of doing something that makes you happy. You can write, read, sing, create content, exercise, paint, dance, cook, play music, take photographs, build something, learn something new, travel, play a sport, spend time in nature, or do anything else that gives you a sense of joy and meaning. There is no definition of what an experience should be. If it means something to you, it belongs here.

We humans were not born to only do routine jobs, attend meetings, chase deadlines and move from one task to another. We were born to live, explore and inspire and do everything that makes sense to us. But somewhere between work mode and deadline mode, we have forgotten the things that make us feel alive. There is something different about doing a thing in hobby mode. You do not do it because someone is expecting an outcome from you. You do it because you want to. What kind of life are we living if we are always pressurized by the unnecessary chaos? We are creative beings who were supposed to live in a world of uncertainty, curiosity and imagination, and bring out the colours that life has to offer.

The goal is simple. Commit yourself to 30 days of doing something you love. You can write a blog post about it, or you can keep a private document and log it every day. Follow it meticulously, not for grade sheet, not for appraisal, not for accountability, not for a certificate and not for recognition. Do it because you love it. For these 30 days, let there be one thing in your life that is not about performance, productivity or deadlines. Let it simply be about you and the joy of doing something you love. Perhaps, after 30 days, you may discover that this was not just a project about finding a hobby. It was about finding a small part of yourself that got buried somewhere along the way.

Things work better when you commit publicly. You can fill the form below if you want your name to be published on this site as someone who took the challenge for the year 2026. Everything that you submit in the form will be uploaded here. At the end of the 30 days, you will be asked to reflect on what you felt, what you experienced, and what the journey was like for you.
